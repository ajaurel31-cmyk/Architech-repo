## Description

Take control of your gout with GoutGuard — the smart, AI-powered app designed to help you manage your condition through intelligent food scanning, purine tracking, and comprehensive health monitoring.

**AI FOOD SCANNER**
Simply snap a photo of your food, nutrition label, or restaurant menu and let our AI instantly analyze the purine content. Get color-coded results (Low/Moderate/High/Very High), estimated purine amounts, and safer alternatives for high-purine foods.

**PURINE DATABASE**
Search our comprehensive database of 500+ foods with detailed purine content information. Filter by category, purine level, or search by name. Know exactly what you're eating.

**URIC ACID TRACKER**
Log your uric acid readings and track trends over time with beautiful charts. See at a glance whether you're hitting your target of below 6.0 mg/dL.

**GOUT FLARE LOGGER**
Track flare events with details including affected joints, pain level, duration, triggers, and treatments. Discover patterns in your flares with analytics.

**MEDICATION REMINDERS**
Never miss a dose of your gout medication. Set up reminders for Allopurinol, Febuxostat, Colchicine, and more. Track doses taken and get drug interaction warnings.

**HYDRATION TRACKER**
Staying hydrated is critical for managing gout. Track your daily water intake with quick-add buttons and progress tracking toward your daily goal.

**PERSONALIZED MEAL IDEAS (Premium)**
Get AI-generated low-purine meal suggestions for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks — filtered by your dietary preferences.

**PRIVACY FIRST**
All your health data is stored locally on your device. We never collect or store your personal health information on our servers.
