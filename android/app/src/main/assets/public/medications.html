Important Drug Interactions

Avoid these foods/drinks while taking immunosuppressants:

  • Herbal supplements
  • Green tea (including matcha)
  • All herbal teas (including Earl Grey)
  • Grapefruit (including juice)
  • Pomegranate (including juice)
  • Dragonfruit/pitaya (including juice)
  • Jackfruit
  • Pomelo (including juice)
  • Seville oranges (often in marmalade)
  • Starfruit

These can cause dangerous changes in medication levels in your blood.
